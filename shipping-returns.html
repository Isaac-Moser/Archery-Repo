Shipping & returns

How we ship your vanes, and what we do if something goes wrong. Effective October 20, 2026.

The short version
  • Every order is printed for your arrow. Most ship within 5–7 business days of payment.
  • Shipping is $4.99, or free when your vanes total $40 or more.
  • Damaged, or we printed it wrong? Email us within 14 days of delivery and we'll replace it free.
  • Because every vane is made to your specs, we can't take returns for a change of mind.

Processing time

Nothing is pulled off a shelf. We print each order after you pay, for the shaft size and specs you entered. Most orders ship within 5–7 business days of payment. Need it sooner for a hunt or a shoot? Say so in the order notes and we'll do what we can, but we can't guarantee a date.

Shipping cost and where we ship

  • Flat rate: $4.99 per order.
  • Free shipping when the vanes in your order total $40 or more (before shipping).
  • We currently ship to addresses in the United States only.

We'll email you when your order ships. Delivery time after that depends on the carrier.

Your shipping address

Please double-check the address you enter at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us right away and we'll fix it if the order hasn't shipped. If a package comes back to us because of an incorrect address, we'll work with you to reship it, and you may need to cover the new shipping cost.

Lost or late packages

If your order hasn't arrived when you expected it, email us with your order reference. We'll check with the carrier, and if the package is confirmed lost in transit, we'll reprint and resend your order at no charge.

Damaged vanes

If a vane arrives damaged, email photos of the damage to moserarchery.orders@gmail.com within 14 days of delivery and we'll ship you replacements at no cost.

If it doesn't fit

Email us within 14 days of delivery with your arrow and shaft size.

  • If we printed it wrong, we reprint it free.
  • If the size entered at checkout was off, we'll work out a fix with you.

Returns

Every vane is made to order for one shaft size, so we can't resell it. That means we don't accept returns or give refunds for a change of mind, or for a different color, profile or twist than the one you ordered. Anything damaged, misprinted or lost in shipping is covered above.

Canceling an order

Changed your mind? Email us within 24 hours of placing your order and we'll cancel it and refund you in full, even if we've already started printing. After 24 hours, your order is in production and can't be canceled.

How refunds work

Refunds go back to the card or payment method you used, through our payment processor, Stripe. Once we issue a refund, your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.

Contact

Email moserarchery.orders@gmail.com with your order reference (it's on your order confirmation page).

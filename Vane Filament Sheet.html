Neon dyes and Black

MagentaBlackNeon Red · Green · Orange

Neon dyes and black pigment are added in very small amounts. They do not change how the TPU melts, so they print the same as Yellow within their line.

No setting change

Digital Blue

Digital Blue

A standard blue pigment at a low loading. It behaves like the neon colors in the regular line.

No change vs other regular colors

White

White HS

White uses a heavy load of titanium dioxide pigment, which slightly thickens the melt. A little more heat and flow makes up for it.

+5 °C · +2% flow · verify with fin-thickness check

Regular vs High Speed

All 4 regular colors

Regular TPU melt is about 2× thicker. It needs more heat to bond thin fins and slightly more pressure advance. Your vane speed stays the same.

+5 °C · PA 0.03 · cap 3.0 · density 1.18

Troubleshooting

If a vane looks wrong

Stringing, bubbles, rough or uneven wallsDry the spool first: 70 °C, 7 h. Don't over-dry, it turns brittle.
Gloss or zits near the topFan +15%. Still there: nozzle −5 °C.
Fins split when flexedFan −10% or nozzle +5 °C.
Fin thicker than 0.60 mmFlow = 0.60 ÷ measured thickness.
Blobs only at fin tips or rootsRun pressure advance calibration.
New spool, same colorQuick fin-thickness check. Lots can shift flow 1–2%.

Privacy policy

What information we collect, why, and who we share it with. Effective October 20, 2026.

The short version
  • We collect what we need to make and ship your order: your name, email, shipping address and arrow specs.
  • Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • No ads, no tracking pixels and no analytics on this site. We don't sell your information.

What we collect

When you order: your name, email address, shipping address, order notes, and the specs for your vanes (arrow and shaft size, wraps, profile, twist, color and any custom shape).

When you pay: you enter your card details on Stripe's secure checkout page, not on our site. Stripe tells us the payment went through, along with basic details such as the amount, your email and the last four digits of the card. We never receive your full card number.

When you email us: whatever you choose to send, such as questions, photos or a review.

Saved in your own browser: your cart and the vane you're building are saved in your browser's local storage so they're still there when you come back. They stay on your device and aren't sent to us until you check out. You can clear them any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Automatically: like any website, our hosting provider receives basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) when you visit, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To make, ship and support your order, and to email you about it.
  • To answer your questions and handle replacements or refunds.
  • To keep the business and tax records the law requires.
  • To post photos or reviews only when you've given us permission.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising. We'll only email you about your order or in reply to you.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run the shop, and only what each one needs:

  • Stripe, to process payments.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website and briefly stores your order details so they can be sent to us after you pay.
  • Google, which provides the email inbox where we receive orders and messages. This site also loads its fonts from Google, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • The shipping carrier, which receives your name and address to deliver your package.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

Each of these companies handles information under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

The copy of your order held by our host is deleted automatically within 7 days. We keep order emails and records for as long as we need them to support your order and to meet tax and legal record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. We'll do it unless we're required to keep it, for example for tax records. You can also ask us to take down any photo or review of yours at any time.

Do Not Track

This site doesn't track you across other websites, so browser “Do Not Track” settings don't change how it works.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, and card payments are handled by Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the information we hold to what we need.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top.
